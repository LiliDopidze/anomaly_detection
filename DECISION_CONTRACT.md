# Version 4 decision contract

Frozen before candidate results, 5 October 2026. This is a synthetic research experiment, not an operator pilot. The main goal is event recall subject to scheduled coverage and investigation workload. Failure to find a feasible policy is a no-go result.

The historical verification_v11 run is reproduced at ec89fb14d38b890c3a3d5040df853c7b6305b075, identified by all 17 saved source hashes. Original inputs/truth are exact; derived floats agree at 1e-10; policies and counts are unchanged. Its original 40/15/20/25 split and Combined score are historical only. New work starts at remote 3333e789590ded14dd313ef42ffd32fd073b68a7. Local main and its existing document edits remain untouched.

The new bounded benchmark uses the unchanged current generator, seed 42, 12 entities, 28 days, five-minute cadence. Generator equations, missingness and random streams remain unchanged; scenario size is an explicit bounded pilot, not the historical 3-entity run or the full 96-entity benchmark. Fault injection begins after 57%, so training ends at 55%, development at 75%. Training eligibility is independently checked against injection truth. Previously inspected seed-42 distributions mean final results are a frozen temporal assessment, not new independent evidence. Variance faults have unknown observable onset; report them as excluded from observable-onset matching and separately from physical onset, never silently substitute injection time.

Scope is downstream/upstream received power, individually for statistical/distance routes and as a declared pair for the forest. GPON meanings stay in the adapter. FEC counts/rates remain available for validation but unsupported as residual predictors in this first experiment. Known-normal history is not inferred from motif frequency. Unknown entities abstain.

The initial reference is a median. One daily harmonic is admitted only if blocked training holdout median absolute error improves by at least 10%, with at least three daily cycles and all eight daily phase bins represented. This is training-only baseline selection. No learned context or regime search. Minimum reference support is 100 observations spanning 48 hours; joint support is 100 selected rows spanning 48 hours. Four and eight features use the same ordered channels and training row identities, stride 3, 100 trees, min(256,n) samples/tree, seed 42. No cap beyond the historical 20,000 deterministic rows, used only if required. Window 60 minutes; long window 360 minutes; EWMA half-life 1 hour; CUSUM k=0.25; persistence boundary 2. These are project choices.

Distance candidates: shape and residual-level, each at 30 and 60 minutes (6 and 12 samples). These cover short precursors and one-hour local changes without a large search. Full eligible training banks, no reduction. Query stride 1. Shape standard deviation floor 1e-8 transformed units. Exclude all shared raw observations and require two disjoint references after exclusion. Training quantiles use overlap-excluded matches. Bank never updates.

Each candidate gets training quantiles 0.99 and 0.999 plus one predeclared edge cutoff at training maximum + 0.25 training score standard deviations (at least 1e-6). Deduplicate numeric ties. Recovery threshold is min(training q90, open minus max(1e-6, 10% of open)); openings 1 and 3, closing 3. No further edge extension. U-level/U-shift are separately scored. Select each standalone route before combining. Named combinations are level+shift, level+forest, level+distance, forest+distance, level+forest+distance. Each uses frozen independently selected component policies. Group spans 0 (ungrouped), 30 and 120 minutes, within entity, compatible power symptoms. Fixed anchor and immediate first notification. No cross-entity grouping, raw score fusion, covariance, instantaneous features or clustering.

Operational proposals: recall >=95%; required-lead recall >=95% across all known-impact faults (unknown onset remains a miss in that denominator); minimum lead 30 minutes; any-route coverage >=95%; full-required-capability coverage >=75%; unmatched investigations <=5 per 1,000 scheduled entity-days; median detected delay <=60 minutes; entity time in alarm <=20%. These are research acceptance proposals, not agreed operator limits. Selection first filters workload and coverage, maximises ordinary observable-event recall, then lower delay (ties within five minutes), then fewer routes and shorter total windows, then stable candidate ID. Recall ties within 0.02. If none feasible, save Pareto candidates and a diagnostic comparator, with deployment disabled. The diagnostic choice is never called feasible.

For grouping comparisons, the ungrouped identical enabled policy is the baseline: recall and lead-recall loss <=2 percentage points, coverage loss <=1 point, nuisance reduction >=50% only where baseline nuisance >0. Undefined ratios stay undefined. All trials, including rejected candidates, are retained. No test selection. Final assessment begins only after artifacts, component policies, enabled combination and no-go/go status are frozen and hashed.

Exposure is the union of scheduled monitoring intervals per entity, independent of observations or number of channels. Decisions use a fixed declared deadline (zero latency for this generator). Late data is explicitly excluded from that original decision, with a quality reason; no backfill or notification rewrite. New faults use half-open observable intervals; boundary and ongoing events are separate. Use deterministic maximum-cardinality ordinary and required-lead matchings; duplicates remain workload. Unknown-time intervals are excluded from timestamp truth and disclosed. No event TN or point adjustment.

Uncertainty is paired at independent OLT blocks using the unchanged topology. This bounded pilot contains only one OLT: a defensible block confidence interval is therefore unavailable. Report raw counts and the limitation, not row bootstrap intervals. A multi-OLT field pilot is needed for uncertainty and transfer evidence. Non-optical, relationship-only, repeated-fault and contamination fixtures are separate diagnostics; they never change generator truth.
